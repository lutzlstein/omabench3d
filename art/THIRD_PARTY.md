# Third-party assets

Every asset below is **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**. Nothing CC-BY, paid, or non-redistributable.
Sources are fetched by `tools/fetch-cc0.sh` into `art/cc0/src/` (gitignored). The list is in `art/cc0/assets.txt`.
`art/blender/cc0_kit.py` builds the scene meshes from them, and `Scripts/make_cc0_materials.py` imports the textures, creates the materials and adds LODs.

## License text, quoted from each source

- **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com/license): "Our assets are all licensed as CC0, which is effectively Public Domain even in jurisdictions that do not support the Public Domain."
- **ambientCG** (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/): "All ambientCG assets are provided under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal License."
- **OpenGameArt, RGS_Dev "Free Low Poly Vehicles Pack"** (https://opengameart.org/content/free-low-poly-vehicles-pack):
  - The page says "License(s): CC0".
  - The pack's License.txt says "This asset is under CC0 License … Public domain and free to use on any project, even commercial. Credit is not required."
